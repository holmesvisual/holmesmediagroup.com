Questions

The things people ask before they book.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. The best-performing pieces are usually the product, the place and the people doing their work. If you or your team want to talk to camera, we'll make it easy and cut around the takes you don't like.

What do you need from us on the day?

Access, and someone who can answer questions for the first fifteen minutes. We plan the shots before we arrive, so the day itself needs very little of your time.

Who owns the footage?

You do. The finished pieces are yours to post anywhere. We keep the raw footage safe for a year in case you want more cut from it.

How fast is delivery?

Trial shoot: first reel in 72 hours, all three inside a week. Retainers: the month's reels inside ten working days of the shoot, posted to a calendar you've approved.

Do you run the ads?

We boost the pieces that are already working, from a budget you set and hold. We don't run large ad campaigns; if you need that, we'll work alongside whoever does.

Where do you work?

Hillcrest and the Upper Highway first, then Durban, Umhlanga and the North Coast to Ballito. Pietermaritzburg and the Midlands by arrangement.
