How does it work?

Post often. See what lands. Scale what's working.

About

I'm Dane. I shoot it, edit it and get it watched.

I'm based in Hillcrest. Before I picked up a camera, I built a Facebook page from nothing to three million followers and over a billion views, without spending a rand on ads, by learning what people actually watch. Eight years of editing came out of that.

I came into film through sound. I studied music production at the Academy of Sound Engineering in Johannesburg, which is why my edits run on story and rhythm rather than effects. Questions

The things people ask before they book.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. The best-performing videos are usually the product, the place and the people doing their work. If you or your team want to talk to camera, we'll make it easy and edit around the takes you don't like.

What do you need from us on the day?

Access, and someone who can answer questions for the first fifteen minutes. We plan the shots with you on a short call before we arrive, so the day itself needs very little of your time.

Who owns the footage?

You do. The finished videos are yours to post anywhere. We keep the raw footage safe for a year in case you want more made from it.

How fast is delivery?

Trial shoot: first video in 72 hours, all three inside a week. Retainers: the month's videos inside ten working days of the shoot, posted to a calendar you've approved.

Do you run the ads?

We boost the videos that are already working, from a budget you set and hold. We don't run large ad campaigns; if you need that, we'll work alongside whoever does.

Where do you work?

Hillcrest, Kloof and the Upper Highway first, then Durban, Umhlanga and the North Coast to Ballito. Pietermaritzburg and the Midlands by arrangement.
